What this is assuming

A browser is told nothing about the lens on a live camera stream — getUserMedia reports resolution and frame rate and no optics at all. So this is a guess, and the absolute plane orientation moves about 2° for every 4° this is wrong (m0/README.md §6). A photo carries EXIF, so in photo mode it is read, not guessed.

Relative depth has no metres in it. This is where the scale comes from, so it is also where “how far before it stops” comes from. 40 mm is a table-tennis ball.

With a virtual ball there is nothing of known size in the picture, so the scale falls back to this. It is a much weaker scale than a real ball, and the band is widened accordingly.

Guessed, not measured — and the widest term in the band. Fitting it from the ball’s own motion is v0.2 on the roadmap. Until then the app samples ±40 % around this instead of pretending to know it. Measure mode prints what your table’s friction actually was.
